# Public source definitions and evidence reconciliation

Content reviewed: 2026-11-10. This is an editorial reconciliation of the existing
12 pinned releases, source reviews and validation reports. It introduces no new
scientific admission, payload audit, annotation certification or rights decision.
Original evidence bytes, source locks, execution contracts and published partitions
retain their previous scope. The unresolved questions below remain unresolved.

## Shared definitions

- **Source release:** one pinned provider record, not an independent acquisition
  site. The provider's licence declaration applies at its recorded scope.
- **Reference availability:** native annotations documented for a specific unit
  and target. `partial` states incomplete or qualified support; `derived_only`
  identifies reconstructions rather than independent ground truth. Neither state
  is silently promoted to `documented` or to execution admission.
- **Tasks and inputs:** instances identify trees in scenes; taxonomy labels trees
  at an explicit rank. Within-tree components distinguish wood, foliage and related
  classes; ground/tree scene classes alone do not establish component supervision.
  Spectral inputs are measurements, not another supervised target. Geometry
  diagnostics do not establish benchmark accuracy. The historical `biomass` key
  represents structure-related evidence, including derived QSMs, not certified
  biomass truth. Stable task keys are retained for compatibility.
- **Preparation admission:** an existing executable route for a specific source,
  target combination and bounded selection. Its validation does not certify
  annotation correctness, geographic independence or an article scoring protocol.
- **Counts:** provider descriptions, native audits, admitted selections and
  historical normalized exports have different denominators. Trees, observations,
  scene files, storage blocks and sites must not be added interchangeably.
- **Dates:** the content-review timestamp dates editorial reconciliation.
  `observation_snapshot_at` dates the saved historical workspace. Individual audit
  reports retain their original dates. Matching snapshot metadata is not a fresh
  provider or payload integrity check.

## Current preparation index

The catalogue's generated `preparations` index projects the four enabled graph
routes and the existing six-plot TreeScan joint admission. Sources, the task model
and both source exports use that index. The graph and the joint source registry
remain the scientific authorities; this index grants no additional execution.
The legacy scalar `discovery.preparation` describes a standalone adapter. In the
TreeScan record it explicitly points to the bounded joint route instead.

The independently checked evidence for an enabled route is linked from its
index entry. Provider facts retain provider references; native-audit findings are
identified as audit findings rather than attributed to a provider landing page.
The full catalogue export includes historical relationships, whose contracts
explicitly identify retired implementation evidence and do not enable conversion.
